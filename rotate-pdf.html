How it works

Choose your PDF, pick the angle (and optionally the pages), then click Rotate & download. Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes. Type the pages in the Pages box, for example 2 or 1-3, 5. Leave it empty to rotate every page.

Does rotating lose quality?

No. The pages are turned without re-rendering, so text and images stay sharp.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.
