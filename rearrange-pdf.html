Pages you leave out are removed. Repeat a number to duplicate a page. Use 0 to insert a blank page.

How it works

Choose your PDF, type the new page order, then click Build PDF. Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete a page?

Leave its number out of the order. For a 5-page PDF, 1,2,4,5 removes page 3.

How do I add a blank page?

Type 0 where you want it. For example 1,0,2,3 puts a blank page after page 1.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.
