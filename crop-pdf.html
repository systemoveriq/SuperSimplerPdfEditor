How it works

Choose your PDF, enter the margins to trim, then click Crop & download. Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is the cropped content deleted?

No. It is hidden from view but still in the file. Use Redact in the main editor for sensitive information.

Does it crop every page?

Yes, the same margins are applied to all pages.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.
