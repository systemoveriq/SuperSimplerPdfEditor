Frequently asked questions

Why is no text found?

Scanned PDFs are pictures of pages with no text layer. Run them through an OCR tool first.

Is the layout kept?

Only plain text is extracted. Tables and columns may not keep their layout.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.
