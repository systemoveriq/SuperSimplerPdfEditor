PDF ↔ Word Converter

Convert PDF to Word and Word to PDF in your browser. Your files are never uploaded.

  • PDF → Word keeps every line at its original position with its font, size and color, and keeps images and graphics. Text sits in positioned frames, so it is editable but does not reflow like normal Word text.
  • Word → PDF shows your document as Word lays it out. Fonts that are not installed on your device get replaced, which can shift the layout slightly.
  • Scanned PDFs have no real text. Use “Exact” mode, or run OCR first.
