Frequently asked questions

Which image types work?

JPG and PNG work best. Other browser-supported types such as WebP are converted automatically.

How do I change the order?

Use the ◀ ▶ buttons under each image. Use ↻ to rotate and ✕ to remove.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.
