Frequently asked questions

Is this PDF editor free?

Yes. You can open, edit and save PDFs without creating an account, and no watermark is added.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Editing, merging and splitting happen inside your browser, so your files stay on your device.

How do I edit a PDF online for free?

Click Choose PDF (or drag your file into the window), pick a tool such as Edit text, Highlight, Redact, Draw or Sign, make your changes, then click Save PDF to download the result.

Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?

Yes. Choose the Edit text tool and click a line of text. The editor matches the original font style, color and background where it can, though some fonts may differ slightly.

Can I redact or blur sensitive information?

Yes. Pages that contain a redaction or blur are flattened to an image when you save, so the hidden text can't be selected or copied from the downloaded file.

Can I sign a PDF online?

Yes. Draw your signature and place it anywhere on the page. You can also add stamps, today's date, check marks and your own text.

Can I merge or split PDF files?

Yes. Use Organize to combine several PDFs into one, or to extract pages, split by range, or split into single pages.

Can I edit a scanned PDF?

A scan is a picture of a page, so it has no editable text. You can still highlight, draw, redact, sign and add text on top of it. To turn a scan into selectable text, try our OCR tool.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The editor adapts to mobile screens and supports pinch to zoom.

Which browsers and devices are supported?

Any modern browser, such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iOS.
