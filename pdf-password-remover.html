Good to know

Can this recover a forgotten password?

No. You need to know the password. This tool removes a password you already have; it doesn’t guess or crack passwords.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is processed on your device and never leaves your browser. Closing the tab clears it.

Why does the new file look slightly different?

The unlocked copy is rebuilt from each page as a high-quality image, with the text added as a hidden layer so you can still search and copy it. Form fields, links and bookmarks are not kept. Choose “Maximum” quality for the sharpest result.

What if it says the password is incorrect?

Check Caps Lock and try again with “Show” turned on. Passwords are case-sensitive.
