Drag a window by its title bar, pull its edges to resize, or tile with Columns, Rows or Grid. Press 🔓 on a window to lock its size and position anywhere you like.

Every PDF scrolls on its own. Want them to move together? Press 🔗 on two or more windows – they keep the offset they have right now. 📌 freezes one in place.
