How it works

Choose a fillable PDF, complete the fields shown, then click Save filled form. Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

What if no fields appear?

The PDF is not a fillable form. Use the main editor to add text on top of the page instead.

What does Flatten do?

It turns the filled fields into normal page content so they cannot be changed afterwards.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser, so your files stay on your device.
